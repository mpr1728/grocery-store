# 07. Brand name

Screened: 2026-10-08. Working name is now **Trivarna**, replacing the NearShelf placeholder.

## What this screen is and is not

A brand name is protected by trademark law, not patent law. Patents cover inventions. The check that matters for a name is a trademark search.

What was done: web searches for existing brands and trademark filings, and DNS lookups on domains. What was not done: a search of the official Indian Trade Marks Registry, which sits behind a form that cannot be searched from here. This screen can find obvious conflicts. It cannot clear a name. Clearance needs the registry search below, ideally by a trademark attorney.

## Trivarna

Meaning: "three colours" in Sanskrit. In several Indian languages the word is used for the national tricolour, so it carries an Indian identity without naming a region or a language.

Brand story that fits the store: three colours for three promises. Saffron for the kitchen and spices, white for dairy and staples, green for fresh produce.

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Existing food, grocery, or retail brand called Trivarna | None found in web searches |
| Trademark filings named Trivarna on aggregator sites | None surfaced. This is weak evidence, not a registry result |
| Similar names in food | Triveni (sugar and FMCG brand, plus a pending "Triveni Bhog" filing in Class 30), Trivaan (Class 30 filing), Trivikrama (cold-pressed oils). Different words, but they share the "Triv" start, so an examiner or an owner could raise similarity |
| trivarna.com | Registered and held by a brand marketplace (BrandBucket nameservers), so it is likely for sale at a premium. Price not confirmed |
| trivarna.in | Registered, not in active use |
| trivarnafresh.com, trivarnafresh.in, trivarnamarket.com, trivarnamarket.in | No DNS records, so they looked free. Confirm at a registrar |

Risks specific to this name:

1. **National flag rules.** The word is not the problem. A logo that looks like the national flag is. The Emblems and Names (Prevention of Improper Use) Act, 1950 bars use of the flag, or a close imitation, in trade or in a trademark without government permission, and the Trade Marks Act refuses registration of such marks. A court has held that saffron, white, and green as colours cannot be restricted by the registry, but a flag-like arrangement invites an objection. Design the logo so it does not read as a flag: no three horizontal bands, no wheel.
2. **Second meaning of "varna".** In older Sanskrit usage "varna" also refers to the social classes, and "trivarna" can be read that way. Most people will hear "tricolour", but test the name with a small, varied group before committing.
3. **Descriptive use by others.** Because it is an ordinary word for the tricolour, other businesses can use it descriptively around national holidays. A distinctive logo and a compound name (for example "Trivarna Fresh") give stronger protection than the word alone.

## Names for a wider marketplace (Amazon or Flipkart scale)

Question on 2026-10-08: which Sanskrit names still work if the business grows from grocery into a general marketplace?

What a name needs for that:

- **No category in the name.** Amazon and Flipkart do not say "books". A word meaning grain, harvest, or food (Navasasya, Ritubhoj, Dhanyam) would have to be dropped later.
- **Not descriptive of commerce either.** Words that simply mean "market" or "goods" are weak trademarks, because the law is reluctant to give one company a common trade word.
- **Short, easy to say in any Indian language and in English.**
- **The bare `.com` obtainable**, because a company at that scale needs it.

About 40 Sanskrit candidates with broad meanings (abundance, world, treasure, market, goods) were checked on `.com` and `.in`. Almost every real single word is taken on both: Vipani, Panya, Dravya, Labhya, Kamya, Ishta, Bhuvana, Samagra, Vipula, Sambhara, Purnam, Anantam, Navanidhi.

| Name | Meaning | `.com` and `.in` | Web screen | View |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Trivarna | Three colours | Both held by others; `.com` is for sale | No conflict found | Best fit. Not tied to any category, so it stretches to anything. "Trivarna Fresh" becomes the grocery arm, the way Amazon Fresh sits under Amazon |
| Panyaloka | World of goods | Both not registered | No brand or filing found | Best fully open option. Four syllables, clear meaning, but leans descriptive |
| Krayaloka | World of buying | Both not registered | Not screened separately | "Kraya" is understood in Hindi (as in kray-vikray). Leans descriptive |
| Koshagara | Treasury, storehouse | Both not registered | No brand or filing found | Reads more like a warehouse or finance brand; long |
| Vipanam | Trade, market | Both not registered | Conflict risk | Avoid. "Vipani" has pending Indian filings by another company in the software, retail, and technology classes, and a "Vipana Retail" company exists. Also close to the Hindi word for marketing |
| Sarvapani, Vishvapani | Coined | Both not registered | Not screened | Avoid. "Pani" reads as "water" in Hindi |

Conclusion: the marketplace ambition strengthens the case for Trivarna. The cost is the domain. `trivarna.com` should be priced and bought early, before the brand has any visible traction, with `trivarnafresh.com` and `.in` used from day one either way. The trademark filing should also be wider from the start: add classes 9, 35, 36, 38, 39, and 42 so the name is protected for a marketplace, payments, and logistics, not only food.

## Recommendation

Go with Trivarna as the working name, with "Trivarna Fresh" or "Trivarna Market" as the full brand and domain. It is the only candidate that is short, easy to say across Indian languages, and carries meaning for the whole country.

## Clearance steps before spending on the name

1. Search the official registry (IP India public search) for "Trivarna" using both the wordmark and the phonetic search, in these classes:
   - 29: dairy, meat, processed food, oils
   - 30: staples, flour, rice, spices, batters, bakery
   - 31: fresh fruit and vegetables
   - 32: juices and beverages
   - 35: retail and online retail services
   - 39: delivery and storage
   - 43: prepared food and kitchen services
   - 9: mobile app
2. Check the company name with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, and search FSSAI licence records for businesses trading under the name.
3. Check the app store and play store for apps with the name.
4. Have a trademark attorney give a written opinion, including the similarity question against Triveni.
5. File the word mark and the logo in at least classes 29, 30, 31, 35, and 43. Filing early matters because India gives weight to both first use and filing date.
6. Register the chosen domains, and ask the price of `trivarna.com` only after the trademark position is clear.
